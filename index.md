{: .fs-9 }

## What is this topic?

AI agents built on large language models (LLMs) plan, call tools, and coordinate with other agents over many steps. When such an agent fails a task, the cause is often hidden in a long execution trace: a wrong plan, a misused tool, a misunderstood instruction, or a mistake by one agent that cascades through others. **Fault localization** is the problem of identifying *which agent, which step, and which kind of error* caused the failure — the first step toward debugging and repairing agent systems.
