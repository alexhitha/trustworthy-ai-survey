# Open Problems and Research Opportunities

_Important unresolved questions, with pointers to the papers that raise them. Claims from prior work are distinguished from my own interpretation._

## Raised by prior work

_From Wang et al. (TSE 2026):_

1. **Step-level accuracy is still limited.** Reported results on Who&When range from about 25% to 52%.
2. **Attribution and enhancement are disconnected.** Attribution methods mostly report responsible agents, faulty steps, and root causes, but enhancement techniques mostly still rely on raw traces.
3. **Evaluation is mostly away from production.** Most benchmarks come from controlled environments or synthetic error injection, which may miss the failures seen in real deployments.
4. **Pass or fail is too coarse.** Most methods produce binary failure labels and rely on task-specific oracles, which are scarce in open-ended tasks.
5. **Task success is not the whole story.** An agent can complete a task while violating important constraints or compromising safety.
